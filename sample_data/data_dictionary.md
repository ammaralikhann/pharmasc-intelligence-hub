# Sample Data Dictionary

This folder contains sample datasets used by the PharmaSC Intelligence Hub prototype.

The data is intentionally small and anonymized for portfolio demonstration purposes.

## 1. inventory_backorder_sample.csv

This dataset supports inventory and backorder risk analysis.

### Purpose

Used by the Inventory Agent to identify SKUs with low inventory coverage, backorder risk, high forecast demand, supplier performance issues, and replenishment risk.

### Key Columns

- `sku`: Product identifier
- `national_inv`: Current national inventory quantity
- `lead_time`: Replenishment lead time in days
- `in_transit_qty`: Quantity currently in transit
- `forecast_3_month`: Forecasted demand for the next 3 months
- `forecast_6_month`: Forecasted demand for the next 6 months
- `forecast_9_month`: Forecasted demand for the next 9 months
- `sales_1_month`: Sales quantity in the last 1 month
- `sales_3_month`: Sales quantity in the last 3 months
- `sales_6_month`: Sales quantity in the last 6 months
- `sales_9_month`: Sales quantity in the last 9 months
- `min_bank`: Minimum inventory buffer
- `pieces_past_due`: Quantity past due from supplier or replenishment pipeline
- `perf_6_month_avg`: Average supplier/service performance over 6 months
- `perf_12_month_avg`: Average supplier/service performance over 12 months
- `local_bo_qty`: Local backorder quantity
- `deck_risk`: Risk indicator used for prioritization
- `went_on_backorder`: Whether the SKU went on backorder
- `days_of_supply`: Estimated inventory coverage in days

### Example Agent Questions

- Which SKUs have low or negative days of supply?
- Which SKUs are most likely to go on backorder?
- Which SKUs have high forecast demand but low inventory?
- Which SKUs have supplier performance concerns?

## 2. supplier_network_sample.csv

This dataset supports supplier, logistics, manufacturing, and transportation risk analysis.

### Purpose

Used by the Supplier Risk Agent to identify supplier delay risk, quality risk, logistics bottlenecks, and cost exposure.

### Key Columns

- `product_type`: Product category
- `sku`: Product identifier
- `price`: Product price
- `availability`: Available product quantity
- `number_of_products_sold`: Quantity sold
- `revenue_generated`: Revenue generated by the SKU
- `stock_levels`: Current stock level
- `lead_times`: General lead time measure
- `order_quantities`: Quantity ordered
- `shipping_times`: Shipping duration
- `shipping_carriers`: Carrier used for transportation
- `shipping_costs`: Shipping cost
- `supplier_name`: Supplier identifier
- `location`: Supplier or production location
- `lead_time`: Supplier lead time
- `production_volumes`: Production volume
- `manufacturing_lead_time`: Manufacturing lead time
- `manufacturing_costs`: Manufacturing cost
- `inspection_results`: Quality inspection result
- `defect_rates`: Product defect rate
- `transportation_modes`: Transportation mode
- `routes`: Transportation route
- `costs`: Transportation cost

### Example Agent Questions

- Which suppliers have high lead times?
- Which SKUs have failed inspection results?
- Which routes have high transportation costs?
- Which products have low stock and high supplier risk?
- Which supplier locations may create replenishment delays?

## How the Data Is Used

The sample data is designed to simulate a supply chain planning workflow:

1. CSV files are stored in Amazon S3.
2. Amazon Athena queries the structured data.
3. AWS Lambda retrieves relevant records and builds prompts.
4. Amazon Bedrock analyzes the supply chain context.
5. Specialized agents generate risk summaries and recommended actions.

## Notes

This data is used only for demonstration and portfolio purposes.
